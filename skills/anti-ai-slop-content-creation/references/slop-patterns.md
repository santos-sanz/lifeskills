# AI slop patterns and remedies

## Common signals
- Generic opening that could introduce any topic.
- Abstract praise or inflated claims without a concrete mechanism.
- Repeated “not X but Y” contrasts, stacked triads, or identical paragraph rhythm.
- Buzzword clusters that hide the actor, action, audience, or result.
- Summary language that claims transformation without showing what changed.
- Polished certainty where the source material is partial, personal, or contested.

## Diagnostic questions
- Could this sentence appear unchanged in ten unrelated pieces?
- What concrete noun, verb, observation, or number would make it testable?
- Is the sentence adding information, voice, or useful movement?
- Did the draft introduce a detail that no source supports?

## Remedies
- Start with a real scene, decision, failure, constraint, or reader problem.
- Name the actor and mechanism instead of saying “we need to leverage” or “drive impact.”
- Delete repeated conclusions and let one precise example carry the point.
- Keep a surprising or imperfect detail when it is true and relevant.
- Prefer a qualified claim over a confident sentence that the evidence cannot support.
