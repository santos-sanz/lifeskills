# Voice and evidence discipline

## Source inventory
Classify material before drafting:
- Fact: supplied or verified information that can be attributed.
- Observation: the user's direct experience or a clearly identified observation.
- Inference: an interpretation drawn from facts or observations.
- Unknown: relevant information not available for the draft.

## Voice preservation
- Keep the user's preferred level of directness, rhythm, humor, and specificity.
- Ask before changing a distinctive phrase that carries identity or intent.
- Remove generic polish only when it obscures meaning; do not make every sentence informal.

## Attribution and disclosure
- Preserve the origin of quotes, numbers, examples, and images.
- Mark illustrative examples and placeholders so they cannot be mistaken for evidence.
- Disclose material AI assistance when the channel, employer, client, or law requires it.
- Never claim personal authorship or experience that the user did not provide.
