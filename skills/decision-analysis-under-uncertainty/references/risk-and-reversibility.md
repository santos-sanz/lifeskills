# Risk and reversibility

## Reversibility check
- Reversible: can be stopped or rolled back with limited cost and no lasting harm.
- Partly reversible: can be changed, but creates transition cost, lock-in, or reputation exposure.
- Irreversible: cannot be undone without material loss, safety exposure, or governance approval.

## Risk register
For each material risk capture:
- Trigger and affected outcome
- Likelihood range or reasoned qualitative level
- Impact and time to detect
- Preventive control
- Contingency owner and action

## Decision triggers
- Define the signal, threshold, measurement window, owner, and response before execution.
- Set a review date even when no trigger fires.
- Escalate risks outside the decision owner's authority rather than disguising them as assumptions.
