# Conversation frameworks

## Observation to request
- Observation: describe a specific behavior, event, or missed expectation.
- Impact: explain the effect on work, trust, time, quality, or safety.
- Perspective: invite the other person's context without presuming intent.
- Request: ask for one observable change, support, or decision.
- Agreement: confirm owner, date, success signal, and follow-up.

## Listening moves
- Ask open questions before defending the plan.
- Reflect content and emotion without agreeing to an inaccurate claim.
- Summarize the shared facts and unresolved disagreement.
- Pause when emotions rise; propose a safer continuation or support channel.

## Repair pattern
- Name the impact and take responsibility for the part you control.
- State what will change and how the other person can verify it.
- Invite a boundary or request from the other person.
- Set a follow-up rather than demanding immediate closure.
