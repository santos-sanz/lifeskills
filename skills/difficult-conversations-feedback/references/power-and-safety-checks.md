# Power and safety checks

## Before the conversation
- Who controls evaluation, pay, access, immigration status, workload, or reputation?
- Could direct contact create retaliation, coercion, or physical danger?
- Is a witness, advocate, manager, HR, union, safeguarding lead, or other channel appropriate?
- What information is necessary, and what must remain confidential?

## Safe adjustments
- Choose a private, accessible, and time-bounded format when safe to do so.
- Offer written or supported communication when speaking live creates avoidable risk.
- Do not promise secrecy, protection, or a particular institutional outcome.
- Document only relevant facts through appropriate policy and preserve evidence of serious concerns.

## Escalation
- Immediate danger or abuse requires an appropriate emergency or safeguarding route, not a confrontation script.
- Harassment, retaliation, discrimination, and repeated unsafe behavior require the responsible formal channel.
- If the user is unsure, state the uncertainty and recommend qualified local support.
