# Dependency and capacity controls

## Critical path
- List each dependency, predecessor, owner, due date, and failure consequence.
- Mark the smallest deliverable that can proceed if the dependency slips.
- Do not call a dependency resolved until the receiving workstream confirms it.

## Capacity
- Start from confirmed capacity, not nominal headcount.
- Account for support, incidents, leave, review time, and coordination overhead.
- Use WIP limits to protect the critical path and expose overload early.

## Escalation
- Escalate with context, exact decision or ask, impact of delay, and due date.
- Route legal, safety, security, or governance issues to the responsible authority.
- Record the decision and owner so the same dependency is not reopened without new evidence.
