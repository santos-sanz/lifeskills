# Operating cadence

## Cadence design
- Daily: only for active blockers, critical path changes, or safety-sensitive work.
- Weekly: review outcomes, leading signals, dependencies, decisions, and next commitments.
- Milestone: inspect definition of done, quality, stakeholder acceptance, and resource trade-offs.
- Closeout: record outcome, learning, unresolved risk, and owner for the next phase.

## Metric design
- Leading indicator: changes early enough to alter behavior or allocation.
- Lagging outcome: confirms whether the objective was achieved.
- Every metric needs definition, unit, owner, data source, period, and decision use.

## Review gates
- Continue when the leading signal and quality bar are on track.
- Adjust when the signal is weak but the hypothesis remains plausible.
- Stop or re-scope when a kill threshold, hard constraint, or safety trigger is reached.
